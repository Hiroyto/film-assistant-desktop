EXT. HARBOUR WALL - DAWN

 Gulls wheel over a slate sea. The tide is out and the boats
 lie on their sides in the mud, keels to the sky, ropes
 slack.

 1) Mara walks the wall

 a) she does not look at the boats

                      BOY
                (calling)
          You missed the tide again!

                      MARA
          It missed me.

          It always does.

 CLOSE ON THE GAUGE

                                             CUT TO:

OPENING SCENE

 Nets hang like weather.

                      MARA (V.O.)
                (quiet)

                (beat)
          Forty years.
