INT. TIDE-GAUGE STATION - DAY

The printout is already spread across the cabinet, already marked — three weeks of Nell's biro, circles and arrows and a date written twice.

NELL

(into the landline)

— no, go back. Row eleven.

She waits. Puts a finger on the row so it can't move.

NELL (CONT'D)

That's the two-forty on the ninth. Now pull the copy I sent you the same night.

A pause on the line.

NELL (CONT'D)

They're different numbers.

VOICE (V.O.)

(thin)

They might have been amended.

NELL

They were. Twice more. The fourteenth and the nineteenth.

She turns the sheet over. The back is marked up too.

NELL (CONT'D)

All three amended after midnight. All three under my login.

VOICE (V.O.)

You'd have to raise that with —

NELL

I'm raising it with you.

Silence. The kind where the other person is deciding what they're allowed to say.

VOICE (V.O.)

Nell. The station's coming off staff at the end of the season. It'll be automatic after that. There's a letter in the post but I'd rather you heard it from a person.

Nell doesn't move. Her finger is still on row eleven.

NELL

That's your answer.

VOICE (V.O.)

It's not an answer, it's a —

NELL

I tell you the record's been altered and you tell me you're getting rid of the person who keeps it.

VOICE (V.O.)

That decision was made in March.

NELL

Then it's a very tidy coincidence.

VOICE (V.O.)

(carefully)

I'd be slow to use a word like that.

The line goes quiet. Nell looks at the marked-up printout — three weeks of her own handwriting, about to become the only copy that says any of this.

NELL

Who else has my login.

VOICE (V.O.)

I'll look into it.

NELL

Who else has it.

VOICE (V.O.)

I'll look into it, Nell.

She hangs up. Stands with her hand flat on the paper, the way you hold something down in wind.

Out the window the water is going out. Ordinary. On time.
