     OVER BLACK.
     A DIESEL ENGINE strains. Tyres grind over loose stone. Rifles
     and webbing knock against metal. A radio HISS. Then-
     The BLAST comes before the picture.
     EXT. KUNAR PROVINCE, AFGHANISTAN - DAY - 2007
     A white flash burns the frame. A pressure wave. Then-
     The lead SNATCH LAND ROVER lifts off the road. Metal tears.
     Earth erupts. The vehicle comes down in pieces.
     The second ROVER locks up twenty meters back.
     The SOUND is muted - as if hearing through cotton.
     CORPORAL “NICK” CALDER, late twenties, Northern, is out
     before the wheels stop.
     Gunfire cracks down from the high ridge.
     Nick throws himself behind a waist-high mud wall. Rounds chew
     through it, showering him in dirt. Five more FUSILIERS pile
     in. Shoulder to shoulder. No room to breathe, never mind
     fight. They fight anyway.
     Nick searches the high ground through the dust. Finds the
     muzzle flashes.
                          NICK
               Contact high left! High left!
     Six rifles swing as one. They open up. The ridge answers.
     Nick looks toward the wreck. Through smoke and falling dirt-
     A man moves on the road.
     LANCE CORPORAL DAN “ASH” ASHWORTH, mid-twenties, East London.
     Five meters short of the burning vehicle. One leg folded
     beneath him. The other torn up.
                          NICK (CONT’D)
               Ash!
     Ash looks back. Their eyes meet.
     Ash: one small shake of the head. Don’t.
     Nick clocks the ground between them. Twenty metres. Open
     road. No cover. He pulls a smoke grenade from his webbing.

                                                            2.2.
                          NICK (CONT’D)
               Smoke out! Keep the ridge down!
     Nick throws the smoke.
                          SERGEANT “HAYES” (O.S.)
               Calder, fucking hold!
     Nick is already over the wall.
     Nick runs low. Dirt snaps against his face. A bullet cracks
     past close enough to turn his head.
     Nick reaches Ash and drops hard beside him.
                          ASH
               Told you to fuck off-?
                          NICK
               You tell me that most mornings.
     Ash catches his wrist as Nick reaches for the drag handle.
                          ASH
               Nick - my legs.
                          NICK
               I know.
                          ASH
               I’m done.
                          NICK
               You got rounds?
                          ASH
               Yeah.
                          NICK
               Then you’re not done.
     Nick grips the drag handle. A beat between them. Then-
     Nick PULLS. Ash SCREAMS - and opens fire.
     Nick dragging. Ash firing. The smoke swallows them.
     A burst rips through it, stitching the road at Nick’s feet.
     He goes down - gets straight back up.
                          NICK (CONT’D)
               Keep them down!
     The mud wall emerges through the smoke. Five metres.

                                                            3.3.
     Ash’s rifle CLICKS empty. His hands won’t close around a
     fresh magazine.
     Nick drops the handle, turns and fires controlled bursts into
     the ridge line. The rest of the section joins him. Six rifles
     hammering the high ground.
     Hands appear through the smoke. Grab Ash by his armour and
     behind the wall. Nick comes over last, landing hard. Straight
     to work.
     He tears a tourniquet from Ash’s kit. Wraps it high around
     the first thigh. Pulls.
     Ash CONVULSES.
                          ASH
               Ah-fuck!
     Nick locks it off and moves to the other leg. Fast. Certain.
     His hands slick with Ash’s blood.
     Ash watches him work. Their eyes meet.
     Nick checks his watch and marks the time on each tourniquet.
     Above them, the firefight begins to recede. Not ending. Being
     taken away. Rifle fire becomes distant thunder.
     The thunder becomes a HISS. And the hiss becomes-
     A KETTLE coming to the boil.
     A PHOTOGRAPH FILLS THE FRAME.
     The same six men beneath the Afghan sun. Hayes at the back.
     Ash at the centre, grinning at something outside the frame.
     Nick beside him, squinting into the light. A small brass
     plaque beneath them:
     THE ROYAL REGIMENT OF FUSILIERS - AFGHANISTAN - 2007
     We pull back. The photograph hangs in a narrow hallway. A man
     stands before it with a mug of tea.
     NICK CALDER, now late forties. He studies the photograph the
     way you study something you no longer need to see to
     remember.
     Nick raises the mug. His inner forearm turns towards us - A
     FUSILIER HACKLE tattoo. The red-and-white plume.
     The kettle CLICKS off somewhere behind him. Nick remains a
     moment longer. Then walks away.
